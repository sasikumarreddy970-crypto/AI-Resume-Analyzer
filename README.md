# 🚀 AI Interview Readiness Analyzer

An AI-powered platform that helps students evaluate their **interview readiness** in under 2 minutes using Resume Analysis, ATS Scoring, Skill Gap Detection, and Machine Learning-based Job Matching.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Every year, millions of students apply for jobs and internships without knowing whether they are truly interview-ready.

Many candidates face:
- Resume rejection due to ATS filtering
- Missing technical skills
- Weak portfolio presentation
- Poor resume structure
- Lack of measurable achievements

Our solution helps students identify these gaps **before facing real recruiters**.

---

# 💡 Solution

The **AI Interview Readiness Analyzer** evaluates resumes against:
- Job Titles
- Job Descriptions
- Required Skills

and provides:
- ✅ Interview Readiness Score
- ✅ ATS Score
- ✅ ML-based Job Match Score
- ✅ Skill Gap Analysis
- ✅ Personalized Recommendations
- ✅ Resume Improvement Suggestions

The entire evaluation process takes **less than 2 minutes**.

## ⚠️ Weakness Detection

## 💡 Personalized Recommendations
Provides actionable suggestions to improve interview readiness.

# 🧪 Machine Learning Approach

The project uses:

## TF-IDF Vectorization
Converts resume and job description text into numerical vectors based on keyword importance.

## Cosine Similarity
Measures similarity between resume and job requirements.

Final Job Match Score is calculated using:
- Semantic similarity
- Keyword matching
- Skill relevance
